Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Pineapple ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Pineapple app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect your phone number for OTP verification, profile information (name, date of birth, gender, city, language, profile photo), call history, and usage data to improve our services. We also collect device information and IP address for security and fraud prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide and improve our services, match you with other users, process payments, send notifications, and ensure platform safety. We do not use your information for advertising to third parties.

3. Audio & Video Calls

Calls are powered by Agora RTC. We do not record any audio or video calls. Microphone and camera access is used solely for real-time voice and video communication between users. You can revoke these permissions at any time from your device settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers who help operate our platform:

  • Agora – Real-time audio/video calls
  • Razorpay – Payment processing
  • MSG91 / SMS provider – OTP delivery

We may disclose data if required by applicable law or legal process.

5. Coins & Payments

Coins are virtual currency used within the app for calls and gifts. They have no real-world monetary value and are non-refundable. Payments are processed securely via Razorpay. We do not store your payment card details on our servers.

6. User Safety

You can report or block any user at any time from their profile. Reported users are reviewed by our moderation team. We reserve the right to suspend or permanently ban accounts that violate our community guidelines.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen. Upon deletion, your personal data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Pineapple is strictly intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately at support@pineappleapp.in and we will delete the account.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@pineappleapp.in or use the account deletion option in the app.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), JWT-based authentication, and secure storage practices to protect your data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app. Continued use of Pineapple after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or concerns, please contact us at:
support@pineappleapp.in
