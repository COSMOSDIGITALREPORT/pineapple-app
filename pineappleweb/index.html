FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about Pineapple.

Pineapple is a premium social calling platform where boys connect with girls through high-quality audio calls. Built for genuine conversations in a safe environment.

Coins are Pineapple's in-app currency. Boys use coins to make calls and send gifts. You can purchase coins via the Wallet screen and use them at any time.

Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks extra calling minutes, a Gold badge, one Fortune Wheel spin, and exclusive rewards. Available for boys only.

The Fortune Wheel gives Premium members one exclusive spin. Prizes range from ₹10 to ₹10,000. Results are fully randomized and completely fair.

After every call, boys can rate on a 1–5 star scale. The overall rating is an average of all received ratings. High-rated girls appear on the weekly Leaderboard.

You can block any user from the Drawer Menu under "Blocked Users." Blocked users can no longer call or connect with you. Unblock anytime.
